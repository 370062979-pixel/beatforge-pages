BeatClock Support

Getting Started

BeatClock is a precision metronome for iOS. Set your tempo, choose your sound, and start practicing.

Free vs Pro

The free version includes a fully functional basic metronome. BeatClock Pro ($4.99 one-time purchase) unlocks all advanced features including polyrhythm, setlists, tempo automation, MIDI sync, and more.

Restore Purchases

If you've already purchased BeatClock Pro, go to Settings and tap "Restore Purchase" to restore your unlock on a new device.

Open Source

BeatClock is based on the open-source project meter-gnome, licensed under the MIT License.

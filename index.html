BeatForge Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Data Collection

BeatForge does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All app data is stored locally on your device.

Analytics

BeatForge does not use any analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking tools.

In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. BeatForge does not have access to your payment information. Purchase status is managed by Apple and stored on your device.

Data Storage

All practice data, settings, and custom sounds are stored locally on your device. No data is sent to any server.

Third-Party Services

BeatForge does not integrate with any third-party services.

Children's Privacy

BeatForge is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page.
